# Implementation Plan - Enhanced Full-Screen Botanical and Geometric Themed Backgrounds

We will upgrade `ThemedBackgroundOverlay.kt` to render rich, highly detailed botanical and geometric motifs across the entire screen for all 12 themes. Each pattern is tuned for optimal visibility and visual harmony in both light and dark modes.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. `ThemedBackgroundOverlay.kt`
- **Red Spider**: Multi-layered spider webs with radial geometric nodes and intersecting delicate web threads.
- **Green Leaf**: Detailed multi-veined botanical leaves, winding stems, and organic foliage clusters.
- **Blue Ocean**: Multi-tier cascading ocean wave crests with hydro-geometric ripple rings.
- **Yellow Volt**: Sharp jagged lightning bolts with electric pulse rings and geometric spark lattices.
- **Purple Cosmos**: Orbital ellipse rings, multi-pointed stars, and cosmic constellations.
- **Orange Fire**: Flickering flame silhouettes, ember circles, and upward thermal curves.
- **Pink Sakura**: 5-petal cherry blossom botanical flowers with stamen centers and floating petals.
- **Cyan Neon**: High-tech grid lattices, circuit nodes, and precise isometric geometric cubes.
- **Teal Jade**: Classical oriental jade bi discs, concentric circles, and delicate floral motifs.
- **Brown Parchment**: Ornate corner filigree, vintage ruling lines, and classical botanical page borders.
- **Indigo Royal**: Regal diamond lattices, crescent moons, and multi-layered crown stars.
- **Slate Noir**: Clean diagonal hatching lines, interlocking hexagonal lattices, and sharp modern geometric lines.
